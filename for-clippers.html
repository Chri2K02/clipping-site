Your Journey

From Application
to First Payout

01
Apply & Get Approved
Complete a short onboarding quiz and submit one sample clip. We review within 48 hours. No experience required — just a sharp eye for viral content.
02
Browse Live Campaigns
Access the NextWave Clippers campaign marketplace. Filter by niche, CPM rate, and content type. Pick campaigns you actually understand and want to clip.
03
Clip, Edit & Post
Download the campaign content, find the best moments, edit your clip (CapCut is free and perfect), and post to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and/or Instagram Reels.
04
Track Views & Get Paid
Submit your clip URL to the campaign dashboard. Views are verified via API within 72 hours. Earnings accumulate in real time and pay out every Friday.
