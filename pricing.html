Your entire $1,000 goes toward verified campaign views. This includes clipper payouts (CPM-based), platform tracking infrastructure, and campaign management. There are no hidden fees or agency add-ons. If your campaign generates 500K views, that's 500K real people who saw your brand's content.

View targets are projections based on campaign performance history — not guarantees. If your campaign significantly underperforms, our team will work with you to diagnose the issue (hook quality, content type, niche fit) and extend the campaign window or offer a partial credit. We're invested in your campaign's success.
YouTube views are verified via the official YouTube Data API — no guesswork. TikTok and Instagram views are tracked using verified third-party scraping services with accuracy checks. We only count organic views — bot traffic is filtered out automatically.
No long-term contracts. Each campaign is a standalone purchase. You agree to our standard campaign terms when submitting, which cover content ownership, usage rights, and platform compliance. That's it. No lock-ins, no retainer commitments unless you want them.
