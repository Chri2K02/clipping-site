Our Mission

Make Viral
Reach
Accessible

Short-form video is the most powerful distribution channel in the world right now. A single clip can put a brand in front of millions of people overnight — at no cost except the time of the creator who made it. But most brands don't have the time, skills, or team to produce short-form content at the volume needed to actually win.

At the same time, there are thousands of skilled creators, editors, and clip artists who are great at producing viral short-form content — but have no reliable way to monetize that skill. They can't easily find brands who need them, and the brands who need them can't easily find them.

NextWave Clippers fixes both sides of that equation. Brands get a frictionless way to launch viral campaigns. Creators get a marketplace of paid campaigns ready to clip. Everyone wins based on actual results — real views, real earnings, real reach.

Our Story

How We Got
Here

NextWave Clippers started from a simple observation: the brands that were growing the fastest on social media in 2023 weren't running expensive ad campaigns. They were going viral through organic short-form video — clips that found the right moment in their content and put it in front of the right audience.

"The brands who won weren't spending more money. They were distributing smarter."
NextWave Clippers was built by a team that understood both sides: the brands that couldn't go viral and the creators who couldn't get paid.

The problem was that producing short-form content at the volume needed to consistently win — 10, 20, 30 clips per week across multiple platforms — was beyond the capacity of any one person or small team. It required a network.

So we built one. NextWave Clippers is a marketplace where brands launch campaigns and verified clippers earn by turning that content into viral short-form clips. The brand gets reach. The clipper gets paid. The content finds its audience.

We started with a handful of brands and 50 clippers in our beta. Within 6 months, campaigns had generated over 10 million verified views. Today, NextWave Clippers has 2,000+ active clippers, dozens of brand partners, and campaigns running across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and X daily.

We're just getting started.
